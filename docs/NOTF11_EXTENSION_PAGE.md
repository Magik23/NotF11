# NotF11

## Any browser tab, toolbar-free.

**Turn it into a clean, resizable window.**

NotF11 removes the browser interface around your current tab—no tab strip, address bar, bookmarks bar, navigation buttons, or extension controls. What remains is the same live page, inside a normal desktop window you can move, resize, maximize, and snap wherever you want.

Unlike F11 fullscreen, NotF11 does not take over your entire display. Your clean window stays part of the desktop, alongside your other windows, apps, and monitors.

One shortcut in. The same shortcut out.

## The same tab, with more room

Press `Ctrl+Shift+F` and NotF11 turns the current tab into a clean window. Press it again and the same tab returns to its original browser window and position.

Because NotF11 moves the live tab instead of reopening the page, your active state stays with it—including your scroll position, entered text, login session, and in-page progress.

## Toolbar-free, still a real window

NotF11 removes the browser controls, not the operating system's window frame. You keep the title bar and normal window controls, so the window remains easy to:

- Move and resize
- Maximize or restore
- Snap beside other applications
- Place on another monitor
- Arrange with Windows Snap Layouts or Microsoft PowerToys FancyZones

It gives the page more space without locking you into fullscreen.

## Built for pages that deserve their own space

Use NotF11 with:

- ChatGPT and other AI tools
- Web apps and dashboards
- Documentation and reference pages
- Browser-based editors and productivity tools
- Video, media, and reading
- Any site you want to treat more like a desktop app

## Default shortcuts

- `Ctrl+Shift+F` — Enter or exit the clean window
- `Ctrl+Shift+,` — Switch to the previous browser tab
- `Ctrl+Shift+.` — Switch to the next browser tab

The previous and next shortcuts let you bring in another tab from the original browser window without leaving clean-window mode. Shortcuts can be changed from your browser's extension-shortcut settings.

## Small, focused, and private

NotF11 does not inject scripts into websites or read or modify page content. It has:

- No analytics or telemetry
- No ads or tracking
- No account or sign-in
- No connection to external services

Its browser permissions are limited to what it needs to manage tabs, windows, keyboard commands, and its own clean-window state.

## Compatibility

NotF11 is designed primarily for Brave on Windows 11 and uses standard Chromium extension APIs. It is also compatible with Google Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers that support those APIs.

## What NotF11 is not

- **Not fullscreen:** it does not occupy or take over the entire display.
- **Not picture-in-picture:** it works with regular webpages, not only video.
- **Not app mode:** no special launch command or separate site installation is required.
- **Not a duplicate page:** it keeps the tab you already have open and active.

Open the page you want. Press `Ctrl+Shift+F`. Keep the page, lose the browser clutter.
